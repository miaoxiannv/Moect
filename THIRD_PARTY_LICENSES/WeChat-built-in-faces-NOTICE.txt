WeChat built-in face artwork

The preset PNG artwork originates from Tencent's public WeChat emoji resources:
https://res.wx.qq.com/t/wx_fed/we-emoji/res/v1.2.8/assets/

Artwork copyright belongs to Tencent and its respective rights holders.
Moect does not claim ownership of this artwork. The application's GPL-3.0
license does not change ownership of the original artwork.

The fixed face-name / resource-address table was cross-checked against:
https://github.com/chenanhan230-del/WeChatMsg-4x/blob/ea4d3f295f767996883958cf88aa5f793cac4c75/app/DataBase/output_pc.py
(GPL-3.0; the existing adapter attribution is retained in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

The current 109-face order and Chinese names were checked against the WeChat
panel and exported token list supplied by the user. ui/wechat-faces.json retains
the original source URL and SHA-256 of each preset file. The image renderer and
portable export integration are implemented in Moect. The preset library is
used to display the corresponding built-in expressions in WeChat archives.
